Парсер и генератор MongoDB ObjectId

A MongoDB ObjectId is 12 bytes: the first 4 bytes are the Unix seconds of creation. Paste one to see its timestamp and fields.

Click "Generate ObjectId" for a well-formed random ObjectId to build local test data.

Features

  • Timestamp recovery — From the first 4 bytes, recover the second-level creation time and show UTC and local time.
  • Field breakdown — Shows machine, process and incrementing counter so you can trace an ID's origin.
  • Local generation — One click generates a well-formed ObjectId without a MongoDB instance.

How to use

  1. Paste a 24-hex ObjectId on the left.
  2. The right side instantly shows the timestamp and fields; a format error is flagged.
  3. Click "Generate ObjectId" to get a new random ID.

FAQ

Is the ObjectId time accurate?

The first 4 bytes are the second-level Unix time of creation, precise to the second; it is client-generated and usually reflects the creation moment.

Does the generated one match the database?

The structure is identical (time plus random machine/process plus counter), but machine and counter are random fill for local testing, not a real instance.
