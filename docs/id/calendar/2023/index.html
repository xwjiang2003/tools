2023 Calendar (with week numbers)

The 2023 calendar shows all 12 months side by side. Every date cell is tagged with its ISO 8601 week number (the "Wk" column on the left), so you can see which week a date belongs to at a glance.

The page is statically generated and can be consulted any time of the year without waiting for scripts to compute.

Features

  • 12 months side by side — Shown month by month — handy for both annual planning and year-end reconciliation.
  • ISO week numbers — Each week is tagged with its ISO week number, matching European enterprise systems.
  • Leap-year aware — 2023 is not a leap year; February has 28 days.

How to use

  1. Just browse this page to see any date and week number in 2023.
  2. Use "Other years" above to switch to an adjacent year.

FAQ

Why do some weeks cross the year boundary?

ISO weeks use the Thursday rule to pick week 1, so week 1 can start in late December of the previous year, and the last week can spill into January of the next.

What does the "Wk" number mean?

It is the ISO 8601 week number of the row (Monday is the first day of the week).
