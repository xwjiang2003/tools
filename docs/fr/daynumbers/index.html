Jour de l'année en cours

Compté depuis le 1er janvier (jour 1) ; une année commune compte 365 jours et une année bissextile 366.

Jour de l'Annee Actuel

Counting from January 1 (day 1), how many days have passed to today — a common year has 365 days, a leap year 366. This day count appears in some log formats, fiscal quarters and astronomical calculations.

The value is computed locally and refreshes each minute.

Features

  • Auto leap year — Leap years (366 days) and common years (365 days) are detected automatically.
  • Live refresh — Updates automatically across day boundaries.

How to use

  1. Open the page to see the current day of the year.

FAQ

Is this the same as a "Julian day"?

No. Here it is "day of the year" (restarting at 1 each year); a Julian day is a continuous count from 4713 BCE, not reset annually, and is used in astronomy and historiography.
